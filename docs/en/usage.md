# Usage examples

[Overview](../../README.en.md) · [한국어](../usage.md)

The examples below describe fictional situations. They are prompts for a connected client, not pre-submitted consultations. Replace them with your own facts and review the final content, fees and consent before submitting. Do not put contact details or other unnecessary personal information into the consultation summary.

## Prepare a request

> My rental agreement in South Korea has ended, but the deposit has not been returned. Help me prepare a Bokdaeri consultation request about recovery options and supporting documents. Show me the final details and costs before I decide whether to submit it.

The assistant can prepare the content; phone verification, required consent and the final web confirmation still apply to this generic MCP connection. Merely sharing this prompt does not skip those steps.

## Check progress

> Check the latest state of the Bokdaeri consultation I just submitted. Tell me whether a lawyer has accepted it and show the lawyer information I am permitted to see.

The client needs access to your consultation. Acceptance is different from a completed call, and a message being scheduled is different from confirmed delivery.

## Edit or withdraw

> Check whether my request can still be edited. If it can, show me a revision changing the amount from five million to fifty million Korean won, and apply it only after I confirm.

> I want to withdraw the request I just submitted. Check whether withdrawal is available, guide me through Bokdaeri's confirmation steps and report the actual result.

Editing and withdrawal are available only before lawyer acceptance, subject to the current service state. Withdrawal ends recruitment and removes the consultation from the lawyer-facing list; it cannot erase information someone has already seen or a notification already delivered.

## If a connection is interrupted

Open [your consultation history](https://mcp.bokdaeri.com/consult) through the service's **내 상담 내역** link and verify the phone number used for that request. Follow the available recovery steps. Check the existing request before creating another one; do not copy authentication cookies between agents or browsers.
